Official Emergency Directory

Pan-India Helplines

Category Service Number Description
National Emergency Pan-India Emergency Helpline 112 Single emergency response number for police, medical, and fire emergencies. ✓ Works offline
Police Police Assistance 100 Immediate police assistance and crime reporting. ✓ Works offline
Medical National Ambulance Service 108 Emergency medical technician response and hospital transit. ✓ Works offline
Medical Alternative Patient Transport 102 Maternity and basic medical transit. ✓ Works offline
Fire Fire Rescue Service 101 Fire accidents, structural collapse, and hazardous rescue. ✓ Works offline
Disaster National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) 1078 Flood, landslide, cyclone, and major disaster rescue. ✓ Works offline
Women Safety Women in Distress Helpline 1091 24/7 dedicated helpline for women safety and emergency support. ✓ Works offline
Women Safety National Commission for Women Helpline 7827170170 Direct round-the-clock emergency support for women. ✓ Works offline
Tourist Support Ministry of Tourism Helpline 1363 Multi-language travel assistance and tourist guidance across India. ✓ Works offline

All numbers above are official Government of India emergency helplines. 112 and 108 are reachable even with minimal network connectivity.

Practical Advisories

Travel with confidence

Always Share Your Live Itinerary

Save your offline itinerary in YatraSetu and share destination checkpoints with trusted contacts before entering low-network regions.

Respect Mountain & Monsoon Signals

If local weather warnings report high precipitation or landslide cautions, choose low-risk valley or cultural alternatives instead of high passes.

Offline-Ready Contacts

Emergency numbers 112 and 108 can often be dialed even with weak cellular coverage or without an active data connection.

Community-Verified Homestays

Whenever travelling solo, select homestays with verified village council or community tourism registration.
